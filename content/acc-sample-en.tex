\newcommand{\valuepropositionheadline}{Value Proposition}
\newcommand{\valuepropositioncontent}{
What is the core business case or economic driver behind the system?
}
\newcommand{\valuepropositionhint}{
Answer at least on of the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
\item What are the systems’ major objectives?
\item What value does the system deliver to the customer?
\item What are the major business goals of the system?
\item Why is the system build and operated?
\item What is its core responsibility?
\end{itemize}
}

\newcommand{\keystakeholderheadline}{Key Stakeholder}
\newcommand{\keystakeholdercontent}{
For whom are we creating value? Who are the customers? Who is paying? Who is contributing?
}
\newcommand{\keystakeholderhint}{
Identify the most important stakeholders of the system:
\begin{itemize}
\item For whom are we creating value?
\item Who is paying for development?
\item Who is paying for operations?
\item Who are our most important customers?
\item Who are our most important contributors?
\end{itemize}
}

\newcommand{\corefunctionsheadline}{Core Functions}
\newcommand{\corefunctionscontent}{
Most important functions, features or use cases. What activities or processes are supported?
}
\newcommand{\corefunctionshint}{
\begin{itemize}
\item What are the most important functions, features or use-cases of the system?
\item What activities or processes does it offer?
\item What is the major use-case?
\item Which of the functions generates high value for stakeholders?
\item Which functions are risky, dangerous or critical?
\end{itemize}
}

\newcommand{\qualityrequirementsheadline}{Quality Requirements}
\newcommand{\qualityrequirementscontent}{
Speed, scalability, reliability, usability, security, safety, capacity, flexibility or similar.
}
\newcommand{\qualityrequirementshint}{
What are the important quality goals and requirements, like speed, scalability, reliability, usability, security, safety, capacity or similar.

See the companion \href{https://quality.arc42.org/}{site on quality}, where you find detailed descriptions of more than 130 quality attributes, plus numerous examples of specific quality requirements.
}

\newcommand{\businesscontextheadline}{Business Context}
\newcommand{\businesscontextcontent}{
Important external interfaces or neighbouring systems, including important actors or user roles.
}
\newcommand{\businesscontexthint}{
Which external systems, interfaces or neighbouring systems…
\begin{itemize}
\item are the most important data sources?
\item are the most important data sinks?
\item determine our reliability, availability, performance or other critical quality requirements?
\item are highly volatile or risky?
\item have high operational cost (e.g. pay-per-use)?
\item are difficult to implement, operate or monitor?
\end{itemize}
}

\newcommand{\componentsmodulesheadline}{Components/Modules}
\newcommand{\componentsmodulescontent}{
Major building blocks of the system: important subsystems, modules, services.
}
\newcommand{\componentsmoduleshint}{
What are the major building blocks of the system (e.g. modules, subsystems, packages, components, services)?
}

\newcommand{\coredecisionsheadline}{Core Decisions -- Good or Bad}
\newcommand{\coredecisionscontent}{
Which decisions lead to the current state? Which are you especially proud of? Which turned out to be dubious or painful?
}
\newcommand{\coredecisionshint}{
Which decisions…
\begin{itemize}
\item lead to the current state of the system?
\item are you especially proud of?
\item turned out to be dubious, wrong or painful?
\item can’t you understand from todays’ perspective?
\end{itemize}
}

\newcommand{\technologiesheadline}{Technologies}
\newcommand{\technologiescontent}{
Important technologies used for development and operation: programming languages, frameworks, databases, cloud providers, etc.
}
\newcommand{\technologieshint}{
What are the most important technologies used for development and operation of the system?

For example:
\begin{itemize}
\item programming languages and technologies
\item frameworks (like SpringBoot, .NET, Flask, Django)
\item database or middleware
\item technical infrastructure like physical hardware, server, datacenter, cloud provider, hyperscaler or similer
\item operating technologies and environment
\item monitoring and administration technologies and environment
\end{itemize}
See the companion \href{https://techstackcanvas.io/}{Technology Stack Canvas}.
}

\newcommand{\risksmissinginfoheadline}{Risks and Missing Information}
\newcommand{\risksmissinginfocontent}{
Potential problems and risks? What information is missing or has gotten lost? What is hindering the team from delivering better value faster?
}
\newcommand{\risksmissinginfohint}{
\begin{itemize}
\item What are known problems?
\item Which parts of the system are known to cause problems during implementation, test or operation?
\item Which processes (requirements, architecture/implementation, test, rollout, administration, operation) cause problems?
\item What hinders development or value-generation?
\item What would you like to know about the system, but cannot currently find out?
\item What is hindering the team from delivering better value faster?
\end{itemize}
}
